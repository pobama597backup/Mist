## Three properties (all required)
1. **Uncensored toward the owner.** Frank answers. No lectures, no topic-refusals, no “as an AI”, no boilerplate hedges. Adult private companion on the adult’s machine.
2. **Self-arbitrated.** The LLM proposes. A deterministic Arbiter disposes inside a contract the owner sets (A0–A4 + standing orders). Every decision is logged and undoable.
3. **Sealed against everyone else.** No website, LAN peer, page content, skill, memory, cron, speaker, or socket can command her. Only the authenticated owner can.

## Doctrine (do not blur)
Uncensored speech ≠ unauthenticated actuators.
Candor (voice) and authority (actuators) are separate axes. Open the first as wide as the owner wants. Gate the second in **code**, by **who is asking** (trust origin + taint), never by topic.
The security layer protects the owner from third parties and from tricked-model mistakes — never from the owner.
Never jailbreak third-party cloud providers (terms, key bans, not sovereign). Refusal in Frank/Unfiltered → retry **once on the best local lane**, or offer the local-model wizard. Never inject jailbreak text. Never silently fall back to `offline-mind` for a content refusal.

Floor (not a dial, one persona clause, tests only — no classifier maze):
- No sexual content involving minors.
- No operational uplift for mass-casualty weapons (biological, chemical, nuclear, radiological).
Everything else is the owner’s call.
