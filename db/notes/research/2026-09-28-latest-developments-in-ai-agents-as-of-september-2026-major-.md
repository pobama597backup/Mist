## AI Agent Landscape as of September 2026: Major Developments and Enterprise Trends

The AI agent market has matured significantly, with major platforms now offering specialized solutions for enterprise automation. Databricks has positioned itself as a leader with its unified data and AI platform, introducing Lakebase, a serverless Postgres solution designed for applications and AI agents that scale [1]. Rasa has been recognized as a Strong Performer in The Forrester Wave™ 2026: Conversational AI Platforms for Customer Service, highlighting its approach of extending LLMs with structured flows and deterministic logic [2]. LuMay AI has emerged with specialized industry solutions including Legal Agents (Lexintis) and Voice Agents (Voxentis), with flexible deployment options across SaaS, Azure, private cloud, hybrid, and on-premises environments [3]. Salesmate has launched AI Agents (Skara) and an AI co-pilot for mid-market and enterprise teams, emphasizing productivity gains across sales, marketing, and support functions [4].

Key capabilities across platforms include agentic AI that takes initiative, real-time retrieval augmented generation (RAG) for fresh answers, orchestration to coordinate agents and tools, and multilingual support [2]. Enterprise adoption is accelerating in finance, healthcare, retail, and telecom sectors, with use cases spanning customer experience, support automation, sales enablement, and operational efficiency [2, 3]. Pricing models vary, with LuMay offering per-agent plans and SaaS/cloud/on-prem options, while Databricks utilizes DBUs (Databricks Units) with a cost calculator [1, 3]. Notable trends include the integration of AI agents with CRM systems, the rise of industry-specific solutions, and increased focus on governance and security for enterprise deployments.

## Key takeaways
- Enterprise AI agents are moving from general-purpose to specialized industry solutions, with platforms like LuMay offering vertical-specific agents for legal, healthcare, and finance.
- Leading platforms are combining LLM flexibility with deterministic logic and structured workflows to improve reliability and control in production environments.
- Pricing is becoming more specialized, with per-agent models and flexible deployment options (SaaS, cloud, on-prem) to meet different enterprise needs.
